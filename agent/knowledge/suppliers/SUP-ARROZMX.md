# SUP-ARROZMX · Arroz Mexicano

## Terms
Rice importer from Mexico. Lead time 14 days (std 3.0), minimum order 1,000 units, 60 day payment terms. Reliability score 0.90. Active.

## Capacity and behaviour
Supplies SKU-RICE-5KG at 2.10 per unit with capacity of 5,000 units a day. The only active rice supplier. Its minimum of 1,000 units is far above what one dark store sells in a month and costs 2,100, more than most stores' grocery budget, so orders often cannot be placed at all. Stores that run short of rice usually get it by transfer from another store instead.
