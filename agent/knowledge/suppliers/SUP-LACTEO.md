# SUP-LACTEO · Lacteos Andinos

## Terms
Dairy supplier based in Colombia. Lead time 5 days (std 1.0), minimum order 200 units, 30 day payment terms. Reliability score 0.94. Active.

## Capacity and behaviour
Supplies SKU-MILK-1L at 0.95 per unit with capacity of 4,000 units a day, so orders are almost never confirmed short. Deliveries are refrigerated and usually on time. The minimum order of 200 is high for a single dark store's milk demand, which is the main reason milk orders end up slightly above the calculated need.
