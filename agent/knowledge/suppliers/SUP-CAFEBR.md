# SUP-CAFEBR · Cafe Brasil Import

## Terms
Coffee importer from Brazil. Lead time 9 days (std 1.5), minimum order 100 units, 45 day payment terms. Reliability score 0.91. Active.

## Capacity and behaviour
Supplies SKU-COFFEE-500G at 4.40 per unit with capacity of 2,000 units a day, so it can absorb large orders that SUP-ANDINA cannot. The long lead time means it only helps if the store can last nine days; for an urgent shortfall it often arrives too late. Used as the alternate coffee supplier.
