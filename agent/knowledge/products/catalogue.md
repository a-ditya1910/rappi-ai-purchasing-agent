# Product catalogue

Handling notes for the products stocked across the Bogota and Mexico City dark stores.

## SKU-MILK-1L · Whole Milk 1L
Dairy, ABC class A. Cases of 12, about 1,100 cm3 per unit. Shelf life 12 days, perishable, stored at 2 to 6 degrees. The fastest moving fresh item, around 55 units a day at Bogota Chapinero, with weekends about 18 percent higher. Over-ordering ends in write-offs, so watch the cover an order creates: up to 1.25 times the shelf life is acceptable, and beyond that POL-PERISH-02 applies. No substitute is listed; customers who find it out of stock usually leave the basket.

## SKU-COFFEE-500G · Ground Coffee 500g
Beverages, ABC class A. Cases of 10, about 900 cm3 per unit. Shelf life one year, not perishable. Steady demand around 42 units a day. Two suppliers: SUP-ANDINA is cheaper and faster but capped at 250 units a day, SUP-CAFEBR is slower and dearer but can ship large quantities.

## SKU-CHIPS-150G · Potato Chips 150g
Snacks, ABC class B. Cases of 24, about 800 cm3 per unit. Shelf life 120 days. Demand historically around 44 a day, but it has been running well above that recently. Bulky for its value, so storage space matters more than cost when ordering large amounts.

## SKU-SODA-2L · Cola 2L
Beverages, ABC class B. Cases of 6, about 2,200 cm3 per unit, the bulkiest product in the range. Shelf life 180 days. Sales jump during supplier promotions and occasionally with a single bulk order from a business customer, so short spikes are common and rarely mean demand has really changed.

## SKU-RICE-5KG · White Rice 5kg
Grocery, ABC class C, a slow mover around 30 units a day. Cases of 4, about 5,200 cm3 per unit. Shelf life 540 days. Hard to buy in small amounts because the only active supplier has a 1,000 unit minimum, so stores lean on transfers. Bogota Usaquen usually holds plenty.
